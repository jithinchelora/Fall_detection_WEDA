How Sound Works

Every sound you hear is a mixture of waves. This explorer lets you see and reshape that mixture in real time.

Step 1
Sound is a mix
Like mixing paint colours, sound is many waves added together — each at a different speed (frequency).
Step 2
FFT separates them
The spectrum chart below is the FFT — it unmixes the sound and shows you exactly which waves are present.
Step 3
Equalizer reshapes it
Each slider removes or amplifies one group of waves. Watch the spectrum change as you move a slider.
