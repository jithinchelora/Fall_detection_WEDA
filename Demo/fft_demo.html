The idea
What your ears hear as a single sound is many sine waves added together — each at a different frequency. The FFT (Fast Fourier Transform) splits that mixed signal back into its components. An equalizer uses this: each slider controls a BiquadFilter that boosts or cuts a specific frequency band before the audio reaches your speakers.
